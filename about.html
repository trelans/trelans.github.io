About the practice

Making games through focused experiments.

This independent portfolio is intentionally centered on the work: the mechanics, systems, visuals, and lessons behind each playable idea.

A practical approach

Build the smallest fun thing first.

The projects range from mobile arcade games to full-stack applications and real-time graphics tools.

What this maker does

The work combines game design and implementation, with hands-on attention to input, feedback, visual hierarchy, level flow, shaders, 2D and 3D assets, and mobile interaction. Unity is the primary game engine, supported by Android, web, and WebGL development when the idea calls for a different surface.

How projects take shape

  • 01 · Find the hookReduce the concept to one interaction that is easy to explain and quick to test.
  • 02 · Prototype the loopBuild the smallest playable version and validate movement, timing, and feedback early.
  • 03 · Create the languageUse color, motion, sound, and interface states to make the rules readable without a manual.
  • 04 · Refine by playingTest the complete loop, remove friction, and keep only the elements that strengthen the idea.

What the archive includes

The project pages document mobile runners, puzzle games, an educational arcade game, Android applications, and a full-stack web prototype. The interactive lab keeps three technical experiments playable in the browser: a 2D paint tool, a keyframe animation editor, and a real-time shading study.

Privacy by design

This public version is deliberately anonymous. Personal details and direct contact information are not published; the portfolio exists to present the games and the craft behind them.
